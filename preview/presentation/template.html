The problem

Gym mein form kaun dekhega?

Most people train alone, and nobody is checking.

Bad form

People lift with poor form and do not notice.

No trainer

A personal trainer is not available or affordable for everyone.

Generic apps

Most fitness apps are not built around Indian gym-goers.

Privacy worries

Cloud video analysis means your workout video leaves your phone.

Budget phones

Heavy apps do not run well on the phones most people own.

The solution

SahiRep — sahi form, sahi tracking

One app. Your phone camera is the coach.

Form coach

Phone camera watches your lift. Live cues and automatic rep count.

Fitness tracker

Log sets, follow a generated plan, track food and progress.

Offline + private

Pose analysis runs on the phone. Works without internet. No video leaves the device.

How it works

From camera to cue, on the phone

Only set history is synced. Video and pose data never go to the server.

Form-coach engine

Judged against your own baseline

22 lifts + squat and push-up

One shared pose core, a rule set per exercise.

Change from your baseline

Compared with how you move, not a textbook body.

Faults only when measurable

A cue is shown only if the camera can actually measure it.

Counting-only mode

For what the camera cannot judge, it counts reps and says nothing about form.

All thresholds are project defaults and still need validation.

Architecture

Offline-first, small sync server

Android app

  • MediaPipe pose on device
  • Room database is the source of truth
  • Everything works with no internet

Flask server

  • JWT authentication
  • Idempotent sync, safe to retry
  • Alembic migrations
Privacy and security

Your video stays on your phone

Done

  • All pose inference on device. No video leaves the phone.
  • JWT with token versioning.
  • Rate limits on the server.

In progress not finished

  • Device-bound sessions.
  • Threat engine.
  • Email OTP for new devices.

Honest limitations

What is not proven yet

  • Not yet validated on real people on real phones.
  • Form thresholds are unverified defaults.
  • Real-phone speed and battery are untested.
  • Postgres / Redis path is untested.
  • Debug build only.
  • Limited data for cooked Indian food.
